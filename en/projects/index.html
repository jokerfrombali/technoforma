What is the difference between installation and installation supervision?

With installation, all facade works are carried out by the company's own crews; with installation supervision, by the client's contractor under the guidance of the manufacturer's specialists. The second option is chosen when the main contractor has its own installers but no experience with the specific system.

  • Installation. Single-point responsibility for the subframe, cladding and programme; the work is done by the company's own installation division of more than 100 specialists.
  • Installation supervision. Training the crew, checking the first work sections, inspecting details and accepting stages; responsibility for carrying out the work remains with the contractor.

The scope of services is described on the construction and installation works page.

How to choose a comparable project for your facade

The comparison should be based not on visual similarity but on the parameters that determine the technology and the organisation of the works. Five criteria are usually enough.

  1. Number of storeys and blocks — these determine the access equipment and the number of crews.
  2. Cladding material and volume — the degree of repetition and the load on production.
  3. Region — seismicity, wind, climate and delivery distance.
  4. Scope of works — design only, design with supply, or the full cycle.
  5. Programme — how tight the schedule was in relation to the volume.

If there is no suitable comparable project, the estimate is made from drawings: elevations, plans and material preferences are sufficient.

What questions should you ask a contractor when looking at its portfolio?

A list of projects is useful if it lets you verify that the contractor has actually carried out works of the required scale. When comparing facade companies, several points are worth clarifying.

  • What part of the work was done in-house: design, production, installation.
  • What volume of cladding was handed over on a single project, and in what time.
  • Whether there is experience in a region with similar climate and seismicity.
  • Who developed the fixing details and carried out author supervision.
  • Whether the working documentation and product samples can be seen.

For each project in this section the dates, volumes and scope of works are given, so most of these questions can be answered before negotiations begin.

Geography of projects: why so many are in the North Caucasus

A significant share of the projects is located in the republics of the North Caucasus and in Stavropol Krai. The region is characterised by active housing construction, seismicity and demand for expressive decoration, which is why lightweight non-combustible GFRC products are in demand here. Working conditions are described in more detail in the section on facades in the North Caucasian Federal District.

The geography of supply is wider, however: production is located in the Moscow region, and the company's warehouses stretch from Novorossiysk and Makhachkala to Yekaterinburg and Novosibirsk.

FAQ

Do the projects include facades that are not made of GFRC?

Yes. On some projects porcelain stoneware and granite were used in addition to GFRC; the volumes for each material are given on the project pages.

Does the company work in regions where it has no projects yet?

Yes. Products are manufactured at the plant in the Moscow region and delivered to site, and on-site works are carried out as installation or installation supervision, depending on the arrangements with the client.

What is needed to estimate a facade by analogy with a completed project?

Architectural drawings or sketches of the elevations, information on the number of storeys and the wall material, and a reference to the project taken as a model. These data are used to determine the range of products and the volumes.
