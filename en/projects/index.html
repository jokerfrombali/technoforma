Our projects

This page brings together major projects where TECHNOFORMA designed the facades and carried out installation or installation supervision: residential complexes in Makhachkala, Grozny and Cherkessk, a club house in Kislovodsk, a hotel complex in Dagestan, a business centre in Moscow and a building of Chechen State University.

What is listed for each project

City, dates of work, number of storeys and blocks, cladding volumes — GFRC, porcelain stoneware, granite — and scope of work: design, installation or installation supervision. The largest by volume are the Dubai Houses residential complex (70,000 m² of GFRC) and Al-Qasr (24,000 m²).
