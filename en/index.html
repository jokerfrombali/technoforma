Who we are

The company has worked with GFRC for more than 10 years and with natural stone for more than 15 years. Its production site near Moscow covers more than 2 hectares, and GFRC production capacity exceeds 20,000 m² per month (company data). The team includes 12 design engineers led by two architects, 3D visualisers and an in-house installation division of more than 100 specialists.

Three lines of business

We work in three areas that can be ordered separately or together — from a sketch to a finished, installed facade.

GFRC

Architectural mouldings and facade panels: cornices, columns, balustrades, window surrounds, pilasters, mouldings, 3D panels and small architectural forms. The material is non-combustible, frost-resistant and allows almost any shape — a modern alternative to architectural concrete, gypsum and expanded polystyrene. GFRC: element manufacturing.

Natural stone

Travertine, limestone, shell limestone (coquina), dolomite, sandstone, marble, granite, basalt, onyx and Dagestan stone. Imported from India, Turkey, China and Iran, supplied to order from anywhere in the world, with layout planning, site measurement and installation. Natural stone: supply and installation.

Construction and installation works

Installation of ventilated rainscreen facade substructures, insulation, cladding with GFRC, natural stone, porcelain stoneware and other materials. Construction and facade works; separately — facade design.

Full cycle under one roof

The design department prepares working documentation and visualisations, our own mould shops make the models and moulds, the plant manufactures the elements with quality control at every stage, logistics deliver them to site, and the installation division fits the substructure, insulation and cladding. The client does not have to coordinate a designer, a factory and an installer — one company is responsible for the facade.

FAQ

How does GFRC differ from architectural concrete?

GFRC is reinforced with glass fibre, so elements can be thin-walled and light while remaining strong. Architectural concrete is a broader term; for facade mouldings and panels GFRC is more practical thanks to its low weight and freedom of form.

How much do architectural concrete and GFRC mouldings cost?

The price depends on the range of elements, complexity of shapes, surface texture, volume and scope of work. We prepare an exact quote based on the project drawings.

Do you make small items such as pillar caps or fireplace surrounds?

Our main focus is building facades, but custom GFRC items can be discussed on request.

Do you work outside Moscow?

Yes, most of our completed projects are in the North Caucasus; supply and installation are possible anywhere in Russia.
