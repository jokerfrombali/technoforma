GFRC Window Surrounds and Frames

Windows are the most repeated part of a facade, so their framing has the greatest influence on the look of a building. TECHNOFORMA Group produces facade decoration from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC): window surrounds, pediments, keystones, sill bands and brackets.

What window framing includes

  • window surround: a profiled frame around the opening;
  • pediment (hood moulding): a small cornice or pediment above the window that sheds water;
  • keystone: the central element of an arch or lintel;
  • sill section: apron, brackets, sill band;
  • reveals: cladding of the side faces of the opening.

Why GFRC

Window surrounds repeat tens and hundreds of times on a facade, so one mould yields many identical products, which makes GFRC cost-effective for residential complexes. The products are light, non-combustible and frost-resistant, are fixed to the wall or to a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure and join neatly with the windows.

Styles

From strict classical profiles to oriental arched surrounds with ornament; such solutions are in demand in North Caucasus projects. For an architect's custom sketch, moulds are made in our own mould shops.

Links with other elements

Window framing is usually designed together with cornices, string courses and bands and pilasters so that the facade divisions align.

Texture, colour and quality

The surface of GFRC products can be smooth, ground, acid-etched or textured to imitate natural stone; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Models and moulds are made in the company's own mould shops, and quality is controlled at every stage, from mixing to packing. Products from one batch are checked against the drawing so that there is no visible difference in tone or geometry on the facade. A sample can be made before series production by agreement.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • product range and repetition of elements: the more identical products, the lower the share of mould costs;
  • profile complexity, depth of relief, ornamentation;
  • size and weight of products, embedded parts and fixings;
  • texture and colour;
  • volume and delivery schedule;
  • scope of works: design, delivery, installation.
  • number of window sizes and complexity of the junction with the window frame.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.

FAQ

Can window surrounds be installed on an existing facade?

Yes, subject to checking the substrate and fixing details; the decision is made after a site inspection.

Do you make arched surrounds?

Yes, including with keystones and ornament.

How does a window surround join the window?

The junction is detailed in the design to prevent leaks and thermal bridges.
