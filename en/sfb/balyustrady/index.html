GFRC Balustrades and Balusters

A balustrade is a railing made of balusters, a handrail and pedestals that adorns balconies, terraces, roofs, stairs and parapets. TECHNOFORMA Group manufactures balusters from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC) and complete balustrade sets for facades and landscaping.

GFRC balusters

Concrete balusters are traditionally heavy; GFRC products are thin-walled and noticeably lighter, yet just as durable outdoors: the material is frost-resistant and non-combustible (Russian fire class NG). This matters on balconies and roofs, where every extra load is accounted for in structural calculations.

Balustrade components

  • balusters: vase-shaped, double-sided, square, ornamented;
  • handrail: the top element of the railing;
  • base rail: the plinth;
  • pedestals (newel posts): supports between spans, often topped with balls or urns.

Railing height and baluster spacing are determined by safety regulations and the purpose of the railing; this is taken into account at the design stage.

Applications

Balconies and loggias of residential buildings, terraces, roof parapets, external stairs and ramps, landscape railings, including together with planters and street furniture.

Balusters we do not make

The company does not produce wooden, metal or wrought-iron stair balusters, only GFRC products for outdoor and facade use.

Texture, colour and quality

The surface of GFRC products can be smooth, ground, acid-etched or textured to imitate natural stone; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Models and moulds are made in the company's own mould shops, and quality is controlled at every stage, from mixing to packing. Products from one batch are checked against the drawing so that there is no visible difference in tone or geometry on the facade. A sample can be made before series production by agreement.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • product range and repetition of elements: the more identical products, the lower the share of mould costs;
  • profile complexity, depth of relief, ornamentation;
  • size and weight of products, embedded parts and fixings;
  • texture and colour;
  • volume and delivery schedule;
  • scope of works: design, delivery, installation.
  • baluster shape and number, span length, pedestals.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.

FAQ

Why are GFRC balusters better than concrete ones?

They are lighter with comparable durability, which reduces the load on balcony slabs and makes installation easier.

Can I buy balusters in Moscow?

Production is located in the Moscow Region; products are made to order from drawings and delivered to site.

Do you make wooden or wrought-iron balusters?

No, only GFRC balusters.

Send us a drawing of the balustrade or facade and we will calculate the set and installation.
