GFRC Facade Columns and Capitals

Columns set the rhythm and scale of a facade, whether at the entrance, a portico, a balcony or along the full height of the building. TECHNOFORMA Group produces facade columns, half-columns, capitals and bases from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC) and installs them on site.

Facade decoration: columns

GFRC facade columns are made as decorative casings around a load-bearing post or as standalone elements for light loads. A thin-walled product is easy to lift and fix, and its surface reproduces the mould texture: smooth, stone-like or fluted.

What a capital is in architecture

In architecture, a capital is the crowning part of a column or pilaster that transfers the load from the entablature to the shaft. Capitals differ by classical order:

  • Tuscan and Doric: restrained, with an echinus and abacus;
  • Ionic: with volutes;
  • Corinthian and Composite: with acanthus leaves, the most complex to make.

A column capital can be ordered separately, for example for restoration or to replace a lost element: we will make it from a survey or a drawing.

What we make

Columns

Cylindrical and with entasis, plain and fluted, round and rectangular; split versions for installation around an existing support.

Capitals and bases

To classical orders or to a custom sketch.

Pilasters and half-columns

Flat vertical elements on a wall; see the pilasters and rustication page.

Texture, colour and quality

The surface of GFRC products can be smooth, ground, acid-etched or textured to imitate natural stone; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Models and moulds are made in the company's own mould shops, and quality is controlled at every stage, from mixing to packing. Products from one batch are checked against the drawing so that there is no visible difference in tone or geometry on the facade. A sample can be made before series production by agreement.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • product range and repetition of elements: the more identical products, the lower the share of mould costs;
  • profile complexity, depth of relief, ornamentation;
  • size and weight of products, embedded parts and fixings;
  • texture and colour;
  • volume and delivery schedule;
  • scope of works: design, delivery, installation.
  • column height and diameter, type of capital.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.

FAQ

Can a GFRC column be load-bearing?

As a rule, a GFRC column is a casing for a load-bearing post; the structural scheme is defined by the design.

Can I order just a capital?

Yes, capitals and bases are made separately to a drawing or survey.

Which orders do you make?

Any, from Tuscan to Corinthian, as well as custom designs.
