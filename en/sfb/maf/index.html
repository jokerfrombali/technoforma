GFRC Street Furniture and Planters

Street furniture and small architectural forms extend the facade into the landscape: planters at the entrance, pots on the terrace, benches and railings in the courtyard. TECHNOFORMA Group makes them from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC) in the same style and texture as the building's decoration.

GFRC planters

Concrete planters are heavy and hard to move; GFRC planters of the same strength are noticeably lighter thanks to their thin reinforced walls. The material is frost-resistant, so large outdoor planters can be left outside over winter.

What we make

Outdoor planters and pots

Classical footed urns, cylinders, cubes, rectangular planters for hedges, in any size.

Landscaping elements

Benches, kerbs, railings, pedestals, decorative spheres, including as a set with balustrades.

Custom designs

To a landscape architect's sketch; we make models and moulds in our own mould shops.

Applications

Entrance areas of residential complexes and business centres, courtyards, restaurant and hotel terraces, public spaces. Using the same material as the facade keeps the style consistent across the whole site, from the cornice to the planter by the porch.

Finishes

The surface can be smooth, textured or stone-like; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Planters for plants are provided with drainage holes.

How to choose the size

Planter size is chosen to suit the scale of the space and the plant: a small pot gets lost at the entrance of a multi-storey building, while a large urn on a narrow terrace gets in the way. Our design engineers help choose sizes and placement according to the landscaping plan and show the result in a 3D visualisation. For trees and shrubs, the soil volume and weight of the filled planter are taken into account; for terraces and roofs, the permissible floor load. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • size and shape of the products;
  • number of identical products in a batch;
  • texture and colour;
  • complexity of decoration;
  • delivery and installation.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.
