GFRC Facade Mouldings and String Courses

Facade mouldings, string courses and profiled bands are linear decoration that ties a facade together: separating floors, framing windows, emphasising the plinth and parapet. TECHNOFORMA Group manufactures exterior facade mouldings from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC).

What a decorative facade moulding is

A moulding is a profiled strip of constant cross-section. On a facade, mouldings frame windows and doors and form frames and panels, while larger profiles are called bands and string courses. It differs from an interior moulding in its material: outdoors, decoration is exposed to frost, sun and precipitation, so a durable non-combustible material is needed.

Types

Facade window mouldings

Frames around openings, a simple alternative to a full window surround.

String courses

Horizontal divisions at floor levels; they mark out the tiers of a tall building and conceal movement joints.

Bands and panels

Profiles for frames on piers, parapets and pediments.

Plinth profiles

Separate the plinth from the wall and shed water.

Why GFRC

Long linear GFRC elements keep their geometry, are frost-resistant and do not burn, unlike coated polystyrene mouldings. The profile is made to the drawing, and the joints between products are detailed so that the line reads as continuous.

What we do not make

The company does not produce interior wall mouldings or car mouldings, only facade decoration.

Texture, colour and quality

The surface of GFRC products can be smooth, ground, acid-etched or textured to imitate natural stone; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Models and moulds are made in the company's own mould shops, and quality is controlled at every stage, from mixing to packing. Products from one batch are checked against the drawing so that there is no visible difference in tone or geometry on the facade. A sample can be made before series production by agreement.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • product range and repetition of elements: the more identical products, the lower the share of mould costs;
  • profile complexity, depth of relief, ornamentation;
  • size and weight of products, embedded parts and fixings;
  • texture and colour;
  • volume and delivery schedule;
  • scope of works: design, delivery, installation.
  • profile cross-section and total running length.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.

FAQ

How are GFRC mouldings joined?

Joints are designed in advance and filled with a compound matching the product colour so that the line is continuous.

Can you make a moulding to our own profile?

Yes, moulds are made to the drawing.

Can I buy mouldings from you in Moscow?

Products are made to order at our production facility in the Moscow Region and delivered to site.

Send us a drawing of the profiles or facade and we will calculate the mouldings and installation.
