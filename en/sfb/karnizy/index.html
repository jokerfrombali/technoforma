GFRC Facade Cornices

A cornice crowns a building and defines its silhouette. TECHNOFORMA Group manufactures facade cornices from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC), from simple profiles to multi-tier crowning cornices with modillions and dentils, and installs them on site.

Exterior facade cornices

Facade cornices do more than shape appearance: a crowning cornice sheds water away from the wall, a string course divides a tall volume into legible tiers, and cornices above and below windows protect the reveals. The cornice material therefore has to withstand frost, precipitation and wind loads for years.

A GFRC facade cornice combines what used to come only from stone and concrete, strength and durability, with the low weight of a thin-walled product. The material is non-combustible (Russian fire class NG), frost-resistant and accurately reproduces the mould profile.

Types of cornices

Crowning cornices

The largest elements with a large projection; they are often assembled from several pieces and fixed to the load-bearing structure via embedded parts. Projection and fixing are calculated by our design engineers.

String courses

Horizontal divisions of the facade; see the mouldings and string courses page.

Window head and sill cornices

Part of the window framing system together with window surrounds and pediments.

Decorated cornices

With modillions, brackets, dentils and ornamental inserts, to the architect's sketch.

GFRC or polystyrene

Polystyrene facade cornices are cheaper and lighter, but they are combustible, need a protective coating and are easily damaged. For multi-storey and public buildings, fire safety regulations restrict the use of combustible decoration. GFRC cornices need no coating and keep their geometry.

Facade cornices, not curtain rails

This page covers exterior architectural cornices only. The company does not make curtain rails or ceiling and wall-mounted curtain cornices, single or double.

Texture, colour and quality

The surface of GFRC products can be smooth, ground, acid-etched or textured to imitate natural stone; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Models and moulds are made in the company's own mould shops, and quality is controlled at every stage, from mixing to packing. Products from one batch are checked against the drawing so that there is no visible difference in tone or geometry on the facade. A sample can be made before series production by agreement.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • product range and repetition of elements: the more identical products, the lower the share of mould costs;
  • profile complexity, depth of relief, ornamentation;
  • size and weight of products, embedded parts and fixings;
  • texture and colour;
  • volume and delivery schedule;
  • scope of works: design, delivery, installation.
  • cornice projection and method of fixing to the wall.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.

FAQ

How much does a GFRC cornice weigh?

The products are thin-walled and therefore much lighter than concrete or stone equivalents; the exact weight depends on the profile and is calculated at the design stage.

Can you make a cornice to our own profile?

Yes, we make moulds in our own mould shops to the architect's drawing.

How is a GFRC cornice fixed?

With embedded parts and brackets to the load-bearing structure or to the ventilated facade substructure; the fixing scheme is part of the design.

Do you sell curtain rails?

No, the company only makes architectural facade cornices.

Send us a profile or facade drawing and we will calculate the cornices and installation.
