GFRC Facade Panels

TECHNOFORMA Group produces facade panels from glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC), flat, textured and 3D, and installs them on ventilated rainscreen facades. These panels make it possible to clad both plain surfaces and complex sculptural facades with a single material.

What GFRC panels are

GFRC panels are thin-walled cladding elements made of glass fibre reinforced concrete that are fixed to a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure. Glass fibre reinforces the panel throughout its volume, so despite its small thickness it is strong and relatively light. The material is non-combustible (Russian fire class NG), which matters when choosing non-combustible panels for the facade of a multi-storey or public building.

Types of panels

Flat

For large-format cladding with a smooth, ground or textured surface.

Textured and 3D

3D facade panels with a relief pattern, ornament or parametric geometry; each mould is made from a digital model.

Stone-textured panels

Reproduce the pattern of natural stone, including rustication.

Corner and reveal panels

For neat corners, reveals and parapets.

Comparison with other panels

Composite, aluminium, metal or linear panel cladding gives a high-tech but flat look. Fibre cement panels are cheaper but limited in form. GFRC facade panels achieve volume, relief and texture close to stone and architectural concrete. They can be combined with porcelain stoneware, as on the Dominanta residential complex, or with FRONTEK, as on RUBLEVO BUSINESS PARK.

Installation

Panels are installed on a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure with insulation and a wind barrier; see the ventilated facade installation page. Insulating the facade of a panel building during refurbishment is handled the same way: insulation beneath the ventilated facade cladding.

Texture, colour and quality

The surface of GFRC products can be smooth, ground, acid-etched or textured to imitate natural stone; colour is achieved with integral pigmentation or painting. Models and moulds are made in the company's own mould shops, and quality is controlled at every stage, from mixing to packing. Products from one batch are checked against the drawing so that there is no visible difference in tone or geometry on the facade. A sample can be made before series production by agreement.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Estimate. We calculate the product range and quantities and agree the scope of works and delivery sequence.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue working documentation, layouts and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipment from the production base in the Moscow Region, via the company's warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists installs the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What affects the price

  • product range and repetition of elements: the more identical products, the lower the share of mould costs;
  • profile complexity, depth of relief, ornamentation;
  • size and weight of products, embedded parts and fixings;
  • texture and colour;
  • volume and delivery schedule;
  • scope of works: design, delivery, installation.
  • panel format, number of unique 3D moulds, substructure type.

We calculate the exact cost from the project drawings.

FAQ

How much do GFRC facade panels cost per m²?

The price depends on format, relief, texture, volume and scope of works; the estimate is based on drawings.

Are GFRC panels non-combustible?

Yes, GFRC is classified as non-combustible (Russian fire class NG).

Can you make 3D panels from our own model?

Yes, moulds are made in our own mould shops from the architect's digital model.

What are the panels fixed to?

To a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure.

Send us drawings or a model of the facade and we will calculate the panels, substructure and installation.
