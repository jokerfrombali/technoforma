About TECHNOFORMA

TECHNOFORMA Group designs, manufactures and installs facades. The company describes itself as the largest manufacturer of GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) in Russia and the CIS.

What we do

We manufacture GFRC elements and panels, supply and install natural stone, and carry out facade construction works: ventilated rainscreen facade substructures, insulation, and cladding with GFRC, stone and porcelain stoneware.

How work on a project is organised

Work on a facade follows a single chain — from the sketch to the installed cladding — and each stage is carried out by the company's own division. This means decisions are coordinated within one team rather than between different contractors.

  1. Input data and estimate. The range of products, the materials and the scope of work are determined from the drawings or a sketch.
  2. Design. The facade design department prepares the visualisation, the product layout and the fixing details.
  3. Models and moulds. The moulding shops make the tooling for each standard size.
  4. Production. The products are cast, gain strength, undergo dimensional checks and are marked.
  5. Delivery. Shipments are made up by work section in the order in which the products will be needed on site.
  6. Installation. The crews install the subframe, the insulation and the cladding.

There is a checkpoint at each transition between stages: the client approves the visualisation and the texture sample before the moulds are started, and the product layout before serial production begins. Changes made at these stages cost far less than remaking finished products or carrying out corrections on the facade.

Which clients and buildings the company works with

The company operates in the B2B segment: its clients are developers, main contractors and architectural practices. A developer is usually concerned with the programme and a predictable budget, a main contractor with tying the facade into the schedule of general building works, and an architect with an accurate rendering of the design in the material.

Completed projects include residential complexes, hotels, business centres and educational buildings. The largest by volume is the Dubai Houses residential complex in Grozny; others include the Al-Qasr hotel complex in Dagestan, RUBLEVO BUSINESS PARK in Moscow, the Dynasty boutique residence in Kislovodsk and the Chechen State University.

Cladding volumes on such projects run to thousands and tens of thousands of square metres, so production and installation are planned in phases. Details of each building — dates, number of storeys, materials and scope of work — are given on the project pages.

Where is the company based and where does it deliver?

The office is in Moscow at 8, 2nd Filyovskaya Street; the factory is in the Moscow Region. From there, GFRC products are dispatched by road to sites in various regions.

The warehouse network stretches from Novorossiysk and Makhachkala to Yekaterinburg and Novosibirsk, which simplifies deliveries of stone and components to remote sites. More about the regions covered can be found in the Geography of Works section.

When planning a delivery to a remote region, the distance to the site, the season of the works and the dimensions of the products are taken into account. Large panels and long cornices are carried in racks and on pallets with the face protected, and the dispatch schedule is tied to the installation sequence.

FAQ

Is it possible to visit the factory?

A visit to the production site in the Moscow Region is arranged in advance through the company's office. On site you can see the moulding shops, texture samples and finished products.

Does the company work with private clients?

The core business is projects for developers and main contractors: residential complexes, business centres, hotels and public buildings. Enquiries for private and boutique houses are considered individually, on the basis of drawings or a sketch.

Does the company publish prices?

No, there is no price list on the website. The cost depends on the product range, the complexity of the shapes, the volume and the scope of work, so the estimate is prepared from the drawings of the specific building.
