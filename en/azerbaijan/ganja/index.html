GFRC and Natural Stone Facades in Ganja

Ganja is Azerbaijan's second largest city, with centuries of history, mosques and caravanserais of fired brick, the Nizami Mausoleum and active modern construction. For residential, public and cultural buildings in Ganja, TECHNOFORMA Group offers facades in GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) and natural stone.

Climate and seismicity

Unlike seaside Baku, Ganja lies in the foothills of the Lesser Caucasus: the climate is moderately warm and more continental, summers are hot, winters bring frost and snow, and freeze-thaw cycles occur more often than on the coast. Wind loads are lower than on Absheron, but the frost resistance of the cladding matters more here. The region is seismically active, so the fixings of the ventilated facade are designed for seismic loads.

According to the company, GFRC has high frost resistance, further improved with additives, and belongs to non-combustibility class NG (Russian classification).

Architecture: brick, stone, ornament

Historic Ganja is red brick: the Shah Abbas Mosque, the caravanserai, bathhouses and houses with brick ornament, pointed niches and portals. Later came buildings in eclectic and neoclassical styles, and the Soviet centre received austere ensembles with colonnades. Modern public buildings and residential complexes often combine these motifs — geometric ornament, arches, portals.

The company makes ornamental panels, screens, arched surrounds and portals in GFRC in its own moulds — in colours and textures agreed with the architect. Natural stone — travertine, limestone, sandstone, granite — suits plinths, entrance areas and the cladding of public buildings.

  • Residential complex — ventilated facade with insulation, GFRC panels combined with porcelain stoneware.

Which stone and cladding to choose for the climate of Ganja

In Ganja, cladding is chosen first of all for its resistance to repeated freezing and thawing. The lower the water absorption of the material and the better water is led away from its surface, the longer the facade lasts.

Facade zoneSuitable materialsComment
Plinth, entrance stepsGranite, basalt, dense limestoneSplash and meltwater zone: minimal water absorption and impact resistance are needed
WallsGFRC, travertine, sandstone, limestone on a subframeThe ventilated cavity removes moisture from behind the cladding
Cornices, string courses, parapetsGFRC with sills and dripsHorizontal surfaces get wettest
Entrance areasStone with a slip-resistant finish, GFRC portalsSafety on steps in frost and rain

For external work, travertine slabs are selected from dense grades with filled pores. How to read the F grade is explained in the article on frost resistance of facade materials.

How oriental ornament is made in glass fibre reinforced concrete

Geometric ornament is built on a repeating module, so a large surface is made up of identical panels cast in a single mould. The architect sets the pattern, and the designers divide it into modules so that the joints coincide with the lines of the design.

Two versions are possible: solid relief on a panel and an open lattice. For lattices the thickness of the bars and the position of the fixing points are critical; they are determined by calculation and checked on a prototype. Pointed arches and portals are assembled from segments on a metal frame. The devices of the style are covered in the article on oriental-style facades; the products themselves are made by the company's GFRC production.

Can GFRC and stone be combined with brick architecture?

Yes, and for Ganja it is a natural combination: a brick wall surface with light-coloured details — niche surrounds, string courses, cornices, portals. The decor is tinted in a warm light or terracotta shade so that it supports the colour of the brick rather than competing with it.

When working with existing masonry, its strength is checked and anchors are selected on the basis of test results. On new buildings the brick appearance is provided by clinker tiles or facing brickwork, while decorative elements are hung on their own fixings to the frame. An example of rich decor on a public building is the Chechen State University in Grozny.

Typical mistakes when choosing a facade for a foothill climate

Most facade problems in a climate with frequent freeze-thaw cycles are built in at design stage. The mistakes usually lie not in the material but in the details where water enters the construction and freezes.

  • Porous stone at the plinth. Soft limestone or shell limestone in the meltwater zone quickly loses its appearance; dense stone is needed here.
  • Horizontal surfaces without a fall. String courses, sills and parapets without flashings and drips cause streaking and delamination.
  • Fully bonded slabs. Large slabs bedded in mortar without a ventilated cavity accumulate moisture behind the cladding.
  • No movement joints. Daily temperature swings and seismic movement need to be accommodated.
  • Fixings without checking the substrate. In old brick masonry, anchors are selected only after pull-out tests.

All these points are resolved in the working drawings of the details, before materials are ordered and moulds are made.

Seasonal working and delivery planning for Ganja

The facade season in Ganja is longer than in central Russia, but breaks are possible in winter because of frost and snow. Panels are hung on the subframe at sub-zero temperatures too, whereas joint sealing, pointing and adhesive works are scheduled for the warm months.

As the products travel a long way, delivery is split into batches by work section, and time for making the moulds is allowed before installation begins. The ways in which the company's installers can be involved are described on the facade works page.

FAQ

Can oriental ornament be made in GFRC?

Yes, models and moulds are made from the architect's sketches in our own workshops.

Will the cladding withstand Ganja's winters?

According to the company, GFRC is frost-resistant; for the plinth the design specifies dense stone — granite or hard limestone.

Do you take Ganja's seismicity into account?

Yes, the substructure and fixings are designed for seismic loads.

Do you have an office in Azerbaijan?

No. Design, production and delivery are arranged from Russia for each specific project; terms are agreed individually.

How much does a facade cost?

The cost is determined after reviewing the project and inspecting the site. Send your drawings to sales@technoforma.ru or call 8 (800) 550-51-98.

Is shell limestone suitable for a plinth in Ganja?

Porous soft stones are not usually recommended for a plinth: in the splash and meltwater zone they deteriorate faster under freeze-thaw cycles. They are used above the plinth and protected with a water repellent, with dense stone placed below.

Can a large open lattice be made?

Yes, large lattices are made up of modules. The size of a single module and the thickness of the bars are determined by calculation, transport conditions and the fixing method.

Should brick samples be supplied to match the colour of the decor?

Preferably. A piece of brick or photographs taken in daylight make it possible to select the pigment on samples before series production starts.
