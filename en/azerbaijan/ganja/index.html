GFRC and Natural Stone Facades in Ganja

Ganja is Azerbaijan's second largest city, with centuries of history, mosques and caravanserais of fired brick, the Nizami Mausoleum and active modern construction. For residential, public and cultural buildings in Ganja, TECHNOFORMA Group offers facades in GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) and natural stone.

Climate and seismicity

Unlike seaside Baku, Ganja lies in the foothills of the Lesser Caucasus: the climate is moderately warm and more continental, summers are hot, winters bring frost and snow, and freeze-thaw cycles occur more often than on the coast. Wind loads are lower than on Absheron, but the frost resistance of the cladding matters more here. The region is seismically active, so the fixings of the ventilated facade are designed for seismic loads.

According to the company, GFRC has high frost resistance, further improved with additives, and belongs to non-combustibility class NG (Russian classification).

Architecture: brick, stone, ornament

Historic Ganja is red brick: the Shah Abbas Mosque, the caravanserai, bathhouses and houses with brick ornament, pointed niches and portals. Later came buildings in eclectic and neoclassical styles, and the Soviet centre received austere ensembles with colonnades. Modern public buildings and residential complexes often combine these motifs — geometric ornament, arches, portals.

The company makes ornamental panels, screens, arched surrounds and portals in GFRC in its own moulds — in colours and textures agreed with the architect. Natural stone — travertine, limestone, sandstone, granite — suits plinths, entrance areas and the cladding of public buildings.

Which solution to choose

  • Public or cultural building — ornamental GFRC decoration and a stone plinth.
  • Residential complex — ventilated facade with insulation, GFRC panels combined with porcelain stoneware.
  • Private house — natural stone cladding with panel layout and 3D visualisation.

Logistics and workflow

Installation: supervision of a local crew, a visit by company specialists or installation by our own teams, by agreement (construction and installation). FAQ

Can oriental ornament be made in GFRC?

Yes, models and moulds are made from the architect's sketches in our own workshops.

Will the cladding withstand Ganja's winters?

According to the company, GFRC is frost-resistant; for the plinth the design specifies dense stone — granite or hard limestone.

Do you take Ganja's seismicity into account?

Yes, the substructure and fixings are designed for seismic loads.

Do you have an office in Azerbaijan?

No. Design, production and delivery are arranged from Russia for each specific project; terms are agreed individually.
