TECHNOFORMA's scope on the project covered design and installation. Design means developing facade cladding solutions by the company's own design department: design engineers and 3D visualisers. Installation is carried out by the company's own installation division.

What a residential complex facade usually requires

A multi-storey residential facade has to combine a distinctive look with durability and fire safety: at this height the cladding is usually fixed to a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure, and decorative elements and panels are made from non-combustible materials. Repeating elements such as window surrounds, string courses and balcony balustrades are cost-effective to cast in series from a single mould, and installation is planned in sections so that it does not hold up finishing works and handover of the building.

The North Caucasus is a seismically active region, so low weight and reliable fixing are especially important for cladding and decorative elements here: lightweight thin-walled GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) products reduce the load on the substructure and the supporting structure.

Facade installation in the winter months: how it is organised

Work on the Belgravia residential complex ran from November to March, that is, during the cold season. For ventilated cladding this is acceptable: the panels are fixed mechanically, without "wet" processes that need temperatures above zero.

In general, winter installation calls for several measures. Sealants and repair compounds are used only within their permitted temperature range, products are stored on spacers under cover, and panel lifting is stopped in strong wind. In a coastal city, wind is a more frequent cause of downtime than frost. More on the limitations can be found in the article on facade works in winter.

What the facade design of a 16-storey building includes

A facade design turns the architectural concept into a set of products and details from which the cladding can be manufactured and installed. For a multi-storey residential building it usually comprises the following parts.

Logistics and installation on a single-block high-rise

On a single block the work front is limited, so the pace is set not by the number of installers but by the sequence in which products are delivered and sections are released. Panels are usually brought in as sets for a storey or a facade area so as not to clutter the site.

Installation proceeds in vertical sections from scaffolding, cradles or mast climbers; the choice depends on the shape of the building and the landscaping around it. First the subframe is set out from a geodetic survey, then the GFRC facade panels and trim elements are hung. Concealed works — bracket fixing, insulation — are accepted before they are covered by cladding. The technology is described in the section on ventilated facade installation.

What matters to the client of a similar residential complex?

For a developer planning a GFRC facade for a residential complex of comparable height, programme and budget are affected most by decisions taken before production begins.

  • Give the designers up-to-date drawings of the frame and window openings.
  • Approve colour and texture samples before the moulds are made.
  • Reduce the number of standard sizes: repetition lowers tooling cost.
  • Coordinate the facade programme with glazing and roofing.
  • Decide in advance who is responsible for details at the interface between design and installation.

Maintaining a GFRC facade after handover

Once the building is commissioned, the facade passes to the managing organisation, which needs clear rules for its care. GFRC cladding does not require seasonal maintenance, but periodic inspections are mandatory.

An inspection covers the condition of joints and sealant, the integrity of panels on the lower storeys and at entrances, and the performance of flashings and rainwater goods. Dirt is washed off with water without abrasives or acids, and minor chips are repaired with a colour-matched compound. Any wall-mounted equipment — air-conditioning units, signs, light fittings — is fixed to the load-bearing wall, not to the panels, and it is useful to write this requirement into the building's rules. More detail is given in the article on caring for GFRC products.

FAQ

Can GFRC panels be installed at sub-zero temperatures?

Yes, mechanical fixing of panels to the subframe does not depend on temperature. The restrictions concern sealants, adhesives and repair compounds, as well as work at height in strong wind and icing.

How long does a facade of this size take?

The duration depends on the complexity of the decor, the number of moulds and the readiness of the building. At Belgravia the work ran from November 2020 to March 2021; for another project the period is set by a programme once the design has been reviewed.

What should be sent for an estimate of a similar facade?

Elevation drawings, plans and sections, information on the load-bearing structure, the site address and preferences for colour and texture. A layout and a preliminary estimate are prepared from these.
