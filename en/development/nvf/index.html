What a ventilated facade is

A ventilated facade is a system in which the cladding is fixed to the wall on a metal substructure, leaving an air gap between the cladding and the insulation. Moisture from the wall and insulation escapes through the gap, while the cladding protects them from precipitation and sun. NVF is the Russian abbreviation for a ventilated rainscreen facade, the standard designation of such systems in Russian design documentation.

Components of a ventilated facade

  • substructure: brackets, rails, fixings made of aluminium, galvanised or stainless steel;
  • insulation: non-combustible mineral wool boards;
  • wind barrier membrane;
  • cladding: GFRC panels, stone, porcelain stoneware, metal cassettes, etc.;
  • fire barriers and junctions with windows, roof and plinth.

Ventilated facade substructure

The substructure is selected to suit the cladding and building height: heavy stone and large GFRC panels need reinforced brackets and closer spacing, while porcelain stoneware uses standard solutions. The substructure is calculated for cladding and wind loads, taking into account anchor test results; an online calculator cannot replace a design engineer here. The substructure manufacturer and type are selected for the specific project.

Regulations

A ventilated facade is designed in accordance with the current Russian building codes (SP) and fire safety requirements; the system requires a technical certificate and a design.

How the work is done

  1. Drawings and brief. A design or facade drawings, photos of the building, cladding requirements.
  2. Estimate. Site inspection, quantities, scope of works; the cost is determined after the inspection.
  3. Design. Design of the substructure, insulation and cladding, load calculations, junction details.
  4. Production and procurement. GFRC products from our own production, stone from the company's stock, substructure and insulation as per design.
  5. Delivery. From the production base in the Moscow Region or via the company's warehouses.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists: substructure, insulation, cladding.

What affects the price

  • area, number of storeys and facade geometry;
  • substructure type and material;
  • cladding type and weight;
  • insulation thickness;
  • condition of the substrate, anchor test results;
  • access method: scaffolding or suspended cradles.

The cost is determined after a site inspection.

FAQ

How much does a ventilated facade cost per m²?

The cost is determined after a site inspection and depends on the cladding, substructure, insulation and number of storeys.

What does NVF mean in construction?

It is the Russian abbreviation for a ventilated rainscreen facade: cladding on a substructure with an air gap over the insulation.

Which substructure should I choose?

It is selected by calculation to suit the cladding, building height and wind zone.

Can a ventilated facade be installed in winter?

Yes, a ventilated facade is a dry technology, so work is possible in the cold season subject to safety requirements.
