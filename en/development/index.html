Construction and facade works

TECHNOFORMA carries out facade works in Moscow, the Moscow Region and other regions: design, insulation, installation of ventilated rainscreen facade substructures, and cladding with GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete), natural stone, porcelain stoneware and other materials. The work is done by our own installation division of more than 100 specialists.

What facade works include

Facade works are the full set of operations to build a building's outer envelope: substrate preparation, substructure fixing, insulation and wind barrier, installation of cladding and mouldings, junctions with windows and roof. For a residential complex, business centre or hotel it matters that this work follows a single design and is done by one team — that makes it easier to meet deadlines and take responsibility for the result.

What we do

Ventilated facade installation

Ventilated facade substructures for any cladding — ventilated rainscreen facade installation.

Insulation

Mineral wool board insulation with a wind barrier as part of a ventilated facade — facade insulation.

Cladding

With GFRC of our own production, natural stone, porcelain stoneware and other materials — for example, GFRC and FRONTEK were used at RUBLEVO BUSINESS PARK.

Facade cladding options

The choice of cladding depends on the building's appearance, height, budget and fire safety requirements:

  • GFRC — architectural mouldings and panels, non-combustible, freedom of form;
  • natural stone — durability and a prestigious look;
  • porcelain stoneware — standardised tiles for plain wall areas;
  • combinations: GFRC for mouldings and accents, porcelain stoneware or stone for plain areas — as at the Dominanta residential complex (14,000 m² of GFRC and 13,000 m² of porcelain stoneware).

How the work proceeds

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Quote. We calculate the range of elements and volumes and agree the scope of work and delivery schedule.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue the working documentation, layout and fixing details.
  4. Production. Models and moulds are made in our own mould shops, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipping from the production base near Moscow, via company warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists fits the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What drives the cost

The price of facade works per m² in Moscow, the Moscow Region and other regions is determined after a site inspection. It depends on:

  • area, number of storeys and complexity of the facade geometry;
  • condition of the substrate and anchor pull-out test results;
  • type of substructure and insulation;
  • chosen cladding and amount of mouldings;
  • access method — scaffolding or cradles, and the season;
  • scope of design work.
