What a business centre facade usually requires

A business centre facade is the calling card of its tenants: it is expected to look modern, have precise geometry and need minimal maintenance. As a rule, this is a ventilated rainscreen facade with large-format cladding, where several materials have to be coordinated in terms of joints, colour and junctions with curtain walling.

What makes a combined business centre facade complex

The main difficulty of combined cladding is bringing two materials into one plane and a single joint grid. GFRC and FRONTEK differ in thickness, weight and fixing method, so the junction details are worked out before production begins.

TaskHow it is solved in general terms
A single facade planea common subframe with different bracket and rail projections for each material
Alignment of jointsthe layout is tied to the building axes, floor slab levels and the glazing module
Junctions with glazingreveals, flashings and make-up pieces are designed together with the cladding
Tolerances of the structural framean as-built survey of the walls before the working drawings are issued, adjustment in the brackets

Over an area of about 20,000 m² an error in one typical detail is repeated hundreds of times, so solutions are usually checked on a control mock-up. How working documentation of this level is produced is described in the article on shop drawings for a facade.

How is facade installation organised on two blocks in a city?

On an urban site the facade is installed in work sections and the elements are delivered for a specific area. There is little storage space in dense development, and keeping panels on site for a long time increases the risk of damage.

  • Each panel receives a mark according to the installation drawing; lorries are loaded in the order of installation.
  • The blocks and elevations are divided into sections so that the subframe, insulation and cladding proceed in a flow and the crews are not left idle.
  • Access equipment such as scaffolding, mast climbers and cradles is chosen according to the height and configuration of each area.
  • Lorry access in Moscow generally depends on the permit regime, so delivery times are agreed in advance.

On a 12-storey building a considerable part of the work is done at height, where wind loads increase: the fixing spacing in corner and upper zones is set by calculation. The works on the project spanned several seasons, including winter ones; this is possible thanks to the dry installation method of the ventilated facade.

How quality is controlled and a large-area facade is accepted

On a project of this scale quality is ensured by stage-by-stage control rather than by inspecting the finished surface. Each layer of the facade is accepted before the next one covers it.

  • Substrate: as-built survey, pull-out tests of anchors in representative zones.
  • Subframe: spacing and projection of brackets, verticality of rails, tightening of fixings.
  • Insulation and membrane: tight fit of the slabs, number of dowels, integrity of the wind barrier.
  • Cladding: flatness, width and straightness of joints, match of the shade with the reference sample.

The results are recorded in certificates for concealed works and as-built drawings. For the client this is the basis of the warranty obligations and of future operation: the documents show how each area is constructed and how an individual panel can be replaced if damaged.

What matters to the client of a similar commercial project

The client of a business centre should bring in the facade contractor at a stage when the joint grid and details can still be influenced. This reduces the number of non-standard elements and simplifies coordination with the glazing.

  1. Hand over the architectural solutions, the structure and the glazing scheme to facade design as a single package.
  2. Approve texture and colour samples and, for GFRC facade panels, a reference sample against which batches are checked.
  3. Approve a control mock-up before mass installation.
  4. Decide how the facade will be maintained: access, inspection intervals and the procedure for ventilated facade maintenance.

FAQ

Can GFRC be combined with other cladding materials on one facade?

Yes, this is common practice. The materials are installed on a common subframe, and differences in thickness and fixing are compensated by the design of the brackets and rails; the junction details are developed in the design.

When should the facade contractor be brought into a business centre project?

Preferably at the architectural concept or design documentation stage, before the glazing grid and levels are fixed. The panel layout can then be coordinated with the structure without rework.
