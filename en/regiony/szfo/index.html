GFRC and Natural Stone Facades in the Northwestern Federal District of Russia

The Northwestern Federal District stretches from Kaliningrad on the Baltic to Naryan-Mar in the Arctic. Here a facade has to withstand damp, wind, sea air and northern frosts.

Climate

St Petersburg, Kaliningrad, Pskov and Novgorod have mild but damp winters with thaws and high humidity all year round. Karelia, Vologda and Arkhangelsk have long snowy winters. Komi, the Murmansk Region and the Nenets Autonomous Okrug are subarctic and Arctic, with frosts, blizzards and hurricane-force winds, plus permafrost in the Nenets Okrug. The common requirements are minimal water absorption of the cladding, high frost resistance and fixings designed for wind loads.

Architecture and stone

The district is St Petersburg classicism and Art Nouveau, medieval Novgorod and Pskov, the German heritage of Kaliningrad and the timber architecture of Kizhi and Vologda. It also has its own famous stones: Vyborg rapakivi granite, Putilovo limestone slab, Karelian gabbro-diabase and Ruskeala marble, Novgorod and Pskov flagstone. The company complements them with granite, marble and travertine from India, Turkey, China and Iran.

Logistics

The nearest shipping point is the production site and stone warehouses in the Moscow Region. St Petersburg is about 700 km away via the M-11 motorway; delivery to Kaliningrad and the Nenets Okrug is arranged on an individually agreed route. The portfolio does not yet include completed projects in the district — see the projects page for examples, such as RUBLEVO BUSINESS PARK.
