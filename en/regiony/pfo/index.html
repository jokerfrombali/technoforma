GFRC and Natural Stone Facades: Volga Federal District

The Volga Federal District stretches from Nizhny Novgorod and Kirov to Orenburg and Perm — from the humid Upper Volga to the steppes and foothills of the Urals. The climate is continental everywhere, but in different ways: long snowy winters in the north, dry heat and steppe winds in the south. The architecture varies too: white-stone Kazan, merchant Samara and Saratov, industrial Izhevsk and Perm.

Regions of the district

The district includes:

  • Republic of Tatarstan — Kazan
  • Republic of Bashkortostan — Ufa
  • Nizhny Novgorod Region — Nizhny Novgorod
  • Samara Region — Samara
  • Perm Krai — Perm
  • Saratov Region — Saratov
  • Orenburg Region — Orenburg
  • Udmurt Republic — Izhevsk
  • Chuvash Republic — Cheboksary
  • Mari El Republic — Yoshkar-Ola
  • Republic of Mordovia — Saransk
  • Penza Region — Penza
  • Ulyanovsk Region — Ulyanovsk
  • Kirov Region — Kirov

Supply and working with clients

For these conditions the approach is the same: the facade is designed for the climate of the specific city. A frost-resistant GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) mix, drainage from cornices and string courses, a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure designed for wind and thermal movement, and low-absorption stone on plinths. GFRC suits the historic decoration of the Volga region — window surrounds, cornices, ornament — while natural stone is supplied from the company's warehouse stock from India, Turkey, China and Iran or made to order.

The western part of the district is supplied directly from the production base and stone warehouses in the Moscow Region; the eastern part (Bashkortostan, Perm Krai, Udmurtia, Orenburg Region) also via the company's warehouse in Yekaterinburg.

We work from the client's drawings: facade design, production, delivery, installation or installation supervision — by agreement. Services: GFRC facades, natural stone, design and construction. Completed projects are on the projects page.
