GFRC and Natural Stone Facades in the Central Federal District of Russia

The Central Federal District is home ground for TECHNOFORMA Group: the company's office is in Moscow, and its production base and stone warehouses are in the Moscow Region. From here, GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) elements and natural stone are delivered to all 18 regions of the district.

Climate and facade requirements

The district's climate is moderately continental but varied. In the west (Smolensk, Bryansk) winters are mild and damp, with frequent thaws. In the north-east (Kostroma, Yaroslavl, Ivanovo) they are longer, snowier and colder. In the south, in the Black Earth region (Belgorod, Voronezh, Kursk, Tambov), summers are hot, with winds and icing. The common problem is frequent freeze-thaw cycles, so a facade needs frost resistance, low water absorption and well-designed drainage.

Architecture

The district preserves the white-stone architecture of Vladimir, the classicism of Kostroma, Kaluga and Tver, the constructivism of Ivanovo and the post-war Stalinist Empire style of Voronezh and Moscow. Reconstruction and new buildings in historic settings call for decoration made to bespoke models — a task for GFRC. Local stone is mainly limestone and dolomite (Moscow Region, Staritsa, Tula, Lipetsk); imported granite is used for plinths.

Logistics

Production is located in the Moscow Region, so most regional capitals are 180 to 700 km away by federal highways. This allows elements to be delivered in batches to match the installation schedule. In Moscow the company delivered RUBLEVO BUSINESS PARK.
