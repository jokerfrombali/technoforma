GFRC and Natural Stone Facades in the Southern Federal District of Russia

The Southern Federal District offers very different conditions for a facade: the sea coasts of Kuban and Crimea, the steppes of the Don, the arid Lower Volga and Kalmykia. TECHNOFORMA Group designs, manufactures and installs facades in GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) and natural stone for projects in the district; the company's warehouse in Novorossiysk serves the southern direction.

What we take into account across the district

  • Black Sea coast and Crimea — salty humid air, winds (the bora in Novorossiysk) and seismicity, which is built into the substructure design.
  • Rostov, Volgograd, Astrakhan, Elista — continental climate with heat, steppe winds and winter freeze-thaw cycles.
  • Adygea and the mountainous part of Kuban — humid foothills and the seismically active Caucasus zone.

The architectural context differs too: the neoclassicism of Volgograd and 'white' Sevastopol, the eclecticism of Rostov and Yekaterinodar, Crimean palaces and sanatoriums, the oriental architecture of Elista. The company manufactures decoration for all these tasks in its own moulds.

How the southern climate affects the choice of cladding and fixings

In the south a facade works under strong solar radiation, heat and, on the coast, salt spray. The design therefore checks colour stability, thermal movement and corrosion protection of the metal separately.

Which facades are in demand in resort and urban development?

Two types of project dominate in the district: hotels, serviced apartments and sanatoria on the coast, and multi-storey residential quarters in Krasnodar, Rostov-on-Don and Volgograd. The former need an expressive appearance and resistance to the marine environment; the latter need repeatable elements and a fast installation rate.

Resort architecture often relies on classical devices: colonnades, balustrades and deeply projecting cornices. Such details are made as GFRC elements: they are lighter than stone ones and place less load on cantilevers and balcony slabs. On lower tiers, terraces and entrance areas stone cladding in light varieties such as travertine, limestone and dolomite is appropriate; the material is dispatched from the Novorossiysk warehouse or supplied to order.

Logistics and the season for facade works in the south

The building season in the south is longer than in central Russia, and the main facade works can be carried out almost all year round. The restrictions are related not to frost but to summer heat, wind and the regime of resort towns.

  • In hot weather, work with adhesives, sealants and mortars is moved to the morning and evening hours, and fresh joints are protected from drying out too fast.
  • In resort towns noisy works and lorry traffic are often restricted in the high season, which is taken into account in the delivery schedule.
  • On the coast hoists and cradles are stopped in strong wind, so a time allowance is built into the programme.
  • Panels arrive from the factory in the Moscow Region by road; marking by work section allows them to go straight into installation.

Installation of the subframe, insulation and cladding is carried out as part of the facade works, while the details and layout are prepared by the design department. The geographically closest completed project of the company is the Dynasty club house in Kislovodsk, in the neighbouring North Caucasus District.

What to prepare for a quotation and how the facade estimate is built up

A facade estimate is made up of the elements, the subframe with insulation, delivery and installation; in the south it is noticeably affected by corrosion-resistance and seismic requirements. For an accurate quotation it is useful to state the site conditions together with the drawings.

  • the distance from the sea and the design seismicity of the area;
  • number of storeys, material of the walls and frame, presence of insulation;
  • a list of decorative elements and panels with dimensions, or elevations from which the designer will compile it;
  • requirements for colour and texture, especially for dark and saturated shades;
  • restrictions on working hours and vehicle access if the project is in a resort zone.

On the basis of this information the fixing grade, the bracket spacing and the scope of work are determined; repeated elements reduce the share of mould costs.

FAQ

Which warehouse serves the south?

The company's warehouse in Novorossiysk; for the Caspian area the Makhachkala warehouse can also be used.

How much does a facade cost?

The cost is determined after reviewing the project and inspecting the site.

Does GFRC fade in the southern sun?

Integral colouring with mineral pigments is resistant to ultraviolet light, so the colour changes slowly and evenly. For saturated dark shades, colour stability and the colouring method should be discussed at the sample stage.

Are special fixings needed for projects by the sea?

Yes, for the coastal zone fixings and subframe components with increased corrosion resistance are usually specified. The exact solution is determined by the design, taking into account the distance from the shore.
