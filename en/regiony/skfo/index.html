GFRC and Natural Stone Facades in the North Caucasus Federal District

The North Caucasus is a key region for TECHNOFORMA Group: most of its major projects are here — residential complexes and a hotel complex in Dagestan, Dubai Houses and a Chechen State University building in Grozny, two residential complexes in Cherkessk and the Dinastia club house in Kislovodsk. The company's warehouse in Makhachkala serves the whole district.

Seismicity, mountains and decoration

Almost the entire district lies in the seismically active Greater Caucasus zone, so the ventilated rainscreen facade substructure and panel fixings are designed for seismic loads. Lightweight GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) panels reduce the weight of the cladding, and according to the company the material is non-combustible (Russian class NG) and frost-resistant — important both on the Caspian coast and in mountain areas.

Developers in the region value expressive facades: cornices, arches, columns, oriental ornament. The company makes its own models and moulds and has extensive experience with Dagestan stones and other natural stone.
