Where we work

TECHNOFORMA's production base and stone warehouses are near Moscow, and the company's warehouses stretch from Novorossiysk and Makhachkala to Yekaterinburg and Novosibirsk. This makes it possible to design, supply and install GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) and natural stone facades in different parts of the country. Russia by federal district

Central Federal District

Our production is located here, so logistics are the shortest. The main load on a facade is frequent freeze–thaw cycles. Completed project in Moscow: RUBLEVO BUSINESS PARK.

North-Western Federal District

A humid maritime climate, wind and prolonged thaws call for low water absorption and well-designed drainage; classical mouldings are in demand for historic buildings. Deliveries come from the production site near Moscow.

Southern Federal District

Hot summers, wind, salty air on the coast and seismic activity in some areas, including Crimea. The nearest warehouse is in Novorossiysk.

North Caucasus Federal District

Most of the company's completed projects: Makhachkala, Grozny, Cherkessk, Kislovodsk. Seismic activity, local limestones and dolomites, and a warehouse in Makhachkala.

Volga Federal District

A continental climate with frosty winters and hot summers; large temperature swings test the frost resistance of materials. Delivery from the production site or via Yekaterinburg.

Ural Federal District

Harsh winters, local granites and marbles, and a company warehouse in Yekaterinburg. Insulation and a reliable ventilated rainscreen facade substructure are especially important here.

Azerbaijan

For projects in Azerbaijan the company can supply GFRC elements and natural stone, carry out design and organise installation or installation supervision by agreement. Details on the Azerbaijan page.

How we work with the regions

The client sends drawings, we calculate the range of elements and logistics, our designers issue the documentation, the elements are manufactured near Moscow and shipped to site directly or via the nearest warehouse. Installation by our own team, installation supervision or a specialist site visit — by agreement. Completed projects are on the Our projects page.

How the region affects the choice of facade solution

The same facade design calls for different materials and fixing details in different parts of the country. Four groups of conditions drive the choice: the temperature regime and the number of freeze–thaw transitions, wind and humidity, the seismicity of the site, and the distance from the factory. They are assessed before detailed design begins, because they determine the GFRC mix, the type of stone and the spacing of fixings.

How are products delivered to remote sites?

Products are dispatched from the factory in the Moscow region by road, in batches tied to the installation schedule. This keeps surplus stock off the site and ensures each work section receives elements in the order in which they are hung.

Three things matter on long routes. The first is packaging: GFRC panels and decorative elements are usually carried on pallets or in crates with spacers, and stone slabs in boxes or on A-frames. The second is labelling: every element carries a number from the installation drawing, so the crew wastes no time sorting. The third is contingency: a small allowance for breakage and trimming is normally included in the batch, its size depending on the geometry of the products and the distance.

Natural stone is best collected through the warehouse nearest to the site: this shortens the delivery leg and makes it easier to reorder slabs of a similar tone.

What to prepare for a facade estimate in your region

Facade drawings and the site address are enough for a preliminary estimate. The fuller the input data, the more accurate the schedule of items and the fewer queries at the facade design stage.

  • architectural drawings: elevations, plans, sections and details where available;
  • town and site address, number of storeys and building height;
  • material of the load-bearing walls and floor slabs;
  • preferences for materials, texture and colour;
  • required scope: supply only, installation supervision or turnkey ventilated facade installation;
  • planned start date of the facade works.

If there is no design yet, the estimate is based on sketches and the quantities are refined once the documentation has been issued.

Which working arrangement should be chosen for a site in the regions?

The arrangement depends on how remote the site is and on whether the client has its own facade contractor. One of three options is usually chosen.

  • Full cycle: design, manufacture, supply and installation by the company, giving a single point of responsibility for the result.
  • Supply with installation supervision: a local crew carries out the work, while the company's specialist trains it and checks the key details.
  • Supply only: the products arrive with installation drawings, labelling and fixing recommendations.

It is convenient to decide at the estimate stage, since the scope of the documentation and the dispatch schedule depend on it.

FAQ

Can we order supply only, without installation?

Yes. The company can manufacture and deliver the products together with installation drawings, while the client's contractor carries out the installation. For quality control, installation supervision is available: a specialist visits the site by arrangement.

Do you work in regions where the company has no warehouse?

Yes. GFRC products are dispatched directly from the factory in the Moscow region, so a warehouse in the region is not essential. The route and delivery schedule are agreed at the estimate stage.
