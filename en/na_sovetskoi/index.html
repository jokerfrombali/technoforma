What TECHNOFORMA did

TECHNOFORMA's scope on the project covered design and installation. Design means developing facade cladding solutions by the company's own design department: design engineers and 3D visualisers. Installation is carried out by the company's own installation division.

What a residential complex facade usually requires

A multi-storey residential facade has to combine a distinctive look with durability and fire safety: at this height the cladding is usually fixed to a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure, and decorative elements and panels are made from non-combustible materials. Repeating elements such as window surrounds, string courses and balcony balustrades are cost-effective to cast in series from a single mould, and installation is planned in sections so that it does not hold up finishing works and handover of the building.

The North Caucasus is a seismically active region, so low weight and reliable fixing are especially important for cladding and decorative elements here: lightweight thin-walled GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete) products reduce the load on the substructure and the supporting structure.

What makes the facade of a single 15-storey building different

A single tall block has no reserve in the form of neighbouring buildings: all crews, materials and lifting equipment are concentrated on one wall, and any delay immediately shifts the overall programme. Facade works on such a building are therefore planned by height, in tiers, with deliveries strictly tied to each tier.

Wind load grows with height, especially in corner zones and at the parapet. As a rule, the spacing of the subframe brackets is reduced in these places, and the fixing of the products is verified by a separate calculation. More on how the system is built can be found on the ventilated facades page.

Scaffolding, cradles or mast climbers: what is installation at height carried out from?

The choice of access equipment determines both the speed and the quality of installation. For buildings of around 15 storeys three options are usually considered, and the decision depends on the facade configuration and how constrained the site is.

EquipmentWhen appropriateLimitations
ScaffoldingComplex decoration, many manual operations, work on several tiers at onceSlow to erect, needs ties to the wall and a level base
Suspended cradlesFlat surfaces, panels and linear elementsSensitive to wind, limited load capacity
Mast climbersTall flat walls, heavy productsRequire a prepared base and anchoring of the mast

The options are compared in the article on scaffolding and cradles for facades.

Installing GFRC in the cold season

Rainscreen cladding is a dry technology, so it can also be installed at sub-zero temperatures: brackets, rails, insulation and products are fixed mechanically. The limitations concern the accompanying operations.

  • joint sealing and local touch-up painting are carried out at the temperature permitted for the specific compound;
  • chemical anchors are selected with the substrate temperature in mind;
  • the insulation is protected from getting wet until it is covered by the cladding;
  • work at height is stopped in strong wind and icy conditions.

Practical recommendations are collected in the piece on facade works in winter.

Sequence of works: how a GFRC facade rises up the building

On a tall building, installation is usually carried out in two passes: first the brackets, insulation and rails are fitted over the full height, then the cladding and decoration are hung. This way the crews do not get in each other's way, and the results of the wall survey reach the working drawings in time.

  1. As-built survey of the elevations and adjustment of the layout to the actual deviations of the walls.
  2. Setting out the axes, installing anchors and brackets, sample pull-out tests.
  3. Installing the insulation and rails with a check of the plane.
  4. Hanging panels and large products, then linear decoration and surrounds.
  5. Sealing joints, fitting flashings and parapet copings, dismantling the access equipment.

Deliveries are split by the same tiers. There is little storage space on the site of a single block, so products are brought in small batches, as a rule for the next few days of work.

What the client should check when accepting the facade of a tall building

Acceptance is carried out in stages: first the concealed works — anchors, subframe and insulation — then the cladding and decoration. On crowning elements such as GFRC cornices, water run-off and the fixing of each product are examined separately. The procedure and the list of documents are described in the article on facade acceptance. Another residential building by the company in Cherkessk is the Zolotye Peski complex.

FAQ

Can facade installation start before the building frame is complete?

Technically yes: cladding of the lower floors is sometimes started while the upper ones are still being built. This requires protective canopies, a programme agreed with the main contractor and finished openings on the floors being clad.

How is wind taken into account when fixing decoration on the upper floors?

Wind load is calculated by height and by facade zone. In corner and parapet zones the number of fixing points is usually increased or the bracket spacing reduced; the solution is recorded in the working documentation.

How much access equipment is needed for one block?

It depends on the perimeter, the programme and the architecture of the facade. Several types are usually combined: scaffolding on complex areas and cradles or mast climbers on flat surfaces.
