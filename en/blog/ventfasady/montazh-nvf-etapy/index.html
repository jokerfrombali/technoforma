Ventilated Facade Installation: Stages of Work

Updated: 2026-09-25 · TECHNOFORMA editorial team

Ventilated facade installation is a sequence of work in which each layer covers the previous one: preparation and survey, brackets, insulation and membrane, rails, cladding, acceptance. A mistake made at an early stage cannot be corrected later without dismantling, so quality is determined not by how fast panels are hung but by inspection of concealed works.

The key criterion for choosing a contractor is whether they work to the design and the system's album of technical solutions and hand over concealed works with signed inspection records. This article is useful for clients, general contractors and technical supervisors who plan and accept rainscreen facades on multi-storey and public buildings.

The system itself is described in the Ventilated rainscreen facades section.

Preparation and survey

Work begins long before the first anchor.

  • Design and documents. Rainscreen working drawings with cladding layout, details and substructure calculation; the technical certificate and album of technical solutions for the system; a method statement (PPR) covering scaffolding or cradles and a storage plan.
  • Substrate survey. Wall type, its condition, voids and cracks. For weak substrates — a decision to fix to floor slabs.
  • Anchor pull-out tests. Carried out on site in different zones of the wall; the results are used to confirm anchor type and bracket spacing.
  • Geodetic survey of the facade. Deviations of the wall from vertical and from plane, positions of openings, corners and parapets are recorded. The survey is used to set bracket offsets and draw the installation layout.
  • Setting-out. Reference axes, levels and bracket positions are marked on the wall.

TECHNOFORMA starts with an inspection and measurements: the cost of work is determined after a site inspection, and the cladding layout is based on the building's actual geometry.

Brackets

Brackets are load-bearing elements that transfer the load from the cladding to the wall. They are installed according to the setting-out, starting with reference rows.

  • Holes are drilled for the anchor specified in the design, observing depth and edge distance.
  • A thermal isolation pad is placed under the bracket foot — it reduces the thermal bridge.
  • Load-bearing brackets that carry the cladding weight are placed where the design specifies — often on floor slabs; support brackets resist wind.
  • Bracket offset is selected from the survey so that the facade plane is even once the rails are installed.

After the brackets are installed, technical supervision checks anchor type, spacing and fixing and signs a concealed works record — the brackets will then be partly covered by insulation.

Insulation and membrane

Insulation is mineral wool boards approved for rainscreen facades. They are installed from the bottom up with staggered joints, tight to the wall and to each other, cut around the brackets.

  • With two-layer insulation, the joints of the layers are offset.
  • Each board is fixed with insulation dowels in the number and pattern specified in the design.
  • The wind barrier membrane — if the system includes one — is laid with overlaps and fixed with the same dowels.
  • Insulation is not left exposed to rain: wet mineral wool loses its thermal performance and dries slowly.

Fire barriers are installed at the same time around openings and at the levels specified in the design. They are also accepted with a concealed works record.

Rails

Rails are vertical (sometimes horizontal) profiles on which the cladding is hung. They are installed on the brackets and aligned in one plane using stringlines or a laser level.

  • The rail is fixed rigidly to the load-bearing bracket and through slotted holes to the support brackets, so the profile can expand when heated.
  • Rail joints along the height have a thermal gap; a cladding element must not bridge this joint.
  • Rail spacing is set by the cladding layout.
  • Fasteners — rivets or bolts of materials compatible with the profile.

The rail plane is the last chance to align the facade. Once the cladding is hung it cannot be corrected, so an as-built survey of the rails is done before cladding starts, and deviations are eliminated by adjusting the connections.

Cladding

Cladding is hung from the bottom up, according to the layout, with each element secured by clips, runners, anchors or cast-in fixings — depending on the material.

  • Porcelain stoneware and fibre cement — standard-format tiles and boards; installation is fast.
  • Natural stone — heavy slabs, numbered layout, shade variation control.
  • GFRC — elements to a bespoke layout with factory cast-in fixings; large elements are lifted mechanically.

Reveals, sills, parapet cappings and window junctions are installed at the same time. At TECHNOFORMA, the substructure, insulation and cladding are installed by the company's own installation teams — more than 100 specialists — so elements from its own production fit a substructure designed in the same loop.

Acceptance

Acceptance of a rainscreen facade is not an inspection of the finished facade but the sum of acceptances at every stage.

  • concealed works records: bracket anchoring, insulation and dowels, membrane, fire barriers, rails;
  • anchor test reports;
  • as-built geodetic survey;
  • passports and certificates for materials and products, system documents;
  • cladding check: flatness, width and evenness of joints, fixing of every element, no chips, clean surface.

Defects found are recorded and rectified before the final certificate is signed. Good practice is a selective check by removing a cladding element in a disputed area.

FAQ

What is the installation technology for a ventilated facade?

Preparation and survey, anchor tests, bracket installation, insulation and wind barrier, fire barriers, rails, cladding, acceptance. Each stage of concealed works is accepted with a signed record.

What is a method statement for ventilated facade installation?

A document describing the sequence of operations, materials, equipment, and quality and safety requirements for a specific system. It is developed as part of the construction method statement (PPR).

How do ventilated facade systems from different manufacturers, such as TechnoNICOL, differ?

In the composition of substructure, insulation, membranes and details, as confirmed by the technical certificate. Installation must follow the album of solutions of the chosen system strictly.

Is the method statement different for a metal cassette ventilated facade?

The stages are the same; hanging the cladding differs: cassettes are hung on runners or fixed through the return, with allowance for thermal expansion.

Who accepts a ventilated facade?

The client's technical supervision and the general contractor — based on concealed works records, test reports, as-built survey and inspection of the cladding.
