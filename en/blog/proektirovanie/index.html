Facade design and selection

What is a facade? A facade is the exterior face of a building, and in construction terms the whole outer envelope: cladding, insulation, substructure, windows and ornament. A dimensioned facade drawing shows divisions, levels, materials and details — appearance, cost and schedule all depend on it.

This section has articles on how to choose cladding and compare options, what stages and documents make up a facade design, what shop drawings (KMD) and a facade passport are, how approval of a building's architectural appearance (AGO) works in Moscow, how to write a tender specification, check an estimate and choose a contractor. Separately — facade composition, choosing materials for climate and seismic conditions, facades in the Caucasus, BIM modelling, designer's supervision and acceptance of works.

Readers also come with private questions: how to finish the facade of a bathhouse, a garage or an aerated concrete house, and whether the facade or the windows should come first. We answer these too, with a caveat: the final decision depends on the wall construction and climate.

Turnkey design is covered on the Facade design page.
