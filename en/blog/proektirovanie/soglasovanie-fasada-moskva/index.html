Architectural Appearance Approval (AGO) in Moscow

Updated: 2026-09-25 · TECHNOFORMA editorial team

Facade approval in Moscow is the procedure in which the city's architecture authority reviews and approves the external appearance of a building: its massing, materials, colours and architectural details. In Moscow it has historically been called approval of the architectural and urban planning solution (AGR) and is carried out by Moskomarkhitektura; the Russian Town Planning Code uses the term architectural and urban planning appearance (AGO) of a building. In practice both mean the same thing: without an approved appearance you cannot build a new building or significantly change the facade of an existing one.

The key point for the client is to build the approval into the schedule and choose materials before submitting documents. The materials, colours and articulation fixed in the approved appearance become binding; replacing the cladding on site without re-approval is a violation.

This article is for developers, client-side technical managers, building owners and architects working in Moscow. The exact list of documents, timeframes and procedure are set by current Moscow regulations and the service regulations on mos.ru, and should be checked before submission. More on design is in the section Facade design and selection.

When AGO approval is required

Appearance approval in Moscow is required in typical cases:

  • New construction: capital construction projects for which approval of the appearance is required by city regulations. Certain categories (for example some individual housing, linear and some industrial facilities) may be exempt; this is checked against current rules.
  • Reconstruction that changes the external appearance of a building.
  • Major repairs and facade alterations: replacing cladding, changing colour or glazing, adding new entrances, shopfronts or extra storeys, if this changes the approved appearance.
  • Revision of a previously approved appearance: for example, replacing the cladding material during construction.

For cultural heritage sites and buildings in their protection zones, additional requirements apply, as well as approvals from heritage protection authorities.

The first practical step is to check, using the land plot's town planning plan (GPZU) and current Moscow regulations, which procedure applies to the building.

Documents

The document package for appearance approval generally includes text and graphic materials. The specific list and formatting requirements are set by the service regulations.

The quality of the visualisations and the accuracy of the finishes schedule directly affect the outcome: the architecture authority assesses the appearance from the materials shown in the package.

Material requirements

Cladding materials in an approved appearance are fixed specifically: type, texture, colour. So material requirements are defined at two levels.

Architectural level: the appearance must fit the urban context: building heights, the style of the surroundings, the compositional role of the site. For the city centre and landmark locations, requirements for cladding quality are higher.

Technical level: materials must comply with the current Russian fire safety and loading codes (SP) for the type and height of the building. For rainscreen facades on multi-storey buildings this means non-combustible cladding and systems with confirmed performance.

  • Natural stone, porcelain stoneware and GFRC (glass fibre reinforced concrete; non-combustible class NG according to TECHNOFORMA) are non-combustible materials widely used in Moscow buildings.
  • The finishes schedule identifies each material unambiguously; colour is given from the manufacturer's catalogue or a colour system.
  • Material samples are worth preparing in advance: they help the architect justify the choice and the client make a decision before submission.

Timeframes

The approval timeframe consists of three parts:

  1. Preparing the package: concept, visualisations, elevations, finishes schedule. Depends on the building and how complete the architectural solution is.
  2. Review by the architecture authority: the period is set by the public service regulations; check it on mos.ru on the date of submission, as regulations change.
  3. Revisions following comments: if the appearance is not approved the first time, the package is corrected and resubmitted, and the review period starts again.

A thorough start helps shorten the overall timeframe: accurate visualisations with real materials, a considered colour scheme, no contradictions between elevations and the finishes schedule. Preliminary consultations with the architecture authority on the concept also reduce the risk of refusal.

Changes to the appearance

An appearance approval applies to a specific solution. If the appearance changes during design or construction, you need to assess whether a revision is required.

  • Replacing the cladding material, for example natural stone with porcelain stoneware or GFRC, usually changes the appearance and requires a revision.
  • Changing colour, articulation or the layout pattern also affects the appearance.
  • Changing the manufacturer while keeping the material, colour and texture may not require re-approval, but this is assessed case by case.
  • Changes to an existing building (new entrances, signage, glazing of loggias, replacing cladding during repairs) also fall under the rules on changing the appearance.

Changing the appearance without approval risks enforcement orders and problems when commissioning the building. So if the idea of replacing a material to save time or money arises on site, first assess whether the replacement fits within the approved appearance.

FAQ

Who approves facade appearance in Moscow?

Moskomarkhitektura, the Moscow City Committee for Architecture and Urban Planning. For cultural heritage sites, heritage protection authorities are also involved.

Does replacing cladding during repairs need approval?

If the replacement changes the approved appearance (material, colour, articulation), then as a rule yes. This is determined by the current rules for the specific building.

How long does architectural appearance approval take?

The review period is set by the service regulations and should be checked on mos.ru. Time for preparing the package and possible revisions is added to it.

Which facade materials get approved in Moscow?

What matters most is that the appearance fits the surroundings and meets technical requirements. Natural stone, porcelain stoneware and GFRC are non-combustible materials widely used in Moscow buildings.
