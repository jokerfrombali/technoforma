Natural stone for facades and interiors

Natural stone is the most durable and recognisable facade material, but each stone has its own character: dense granite and basalt stand up to plinths and paving, soft limestone, shell limestone (coquina) and sandstone are easy to work and give a warm texture, travertine and marble suit feature surfaces, and onyx comes alive with backlighting in interiors.

This section has articles on stone properties and comparisons, types of finish and slab thickness, installation methods and plinth cladding, stone protection and dealing with efflorescence, imports from India, Turkey, China and Iran, layout and 3D visualisation before purchase, as well as what makes up the price and how to order stone for a project — from sample to delivery.

A separate article is devoted to Dagestan stone: the limestones and dolomites of the North Caucasus that the company works with extensively.

Supply and installation are covered on the Natural stone: supply and installation page.
