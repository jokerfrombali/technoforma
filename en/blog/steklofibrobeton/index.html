GFRC: material and technology

What is GFRC? GFRC is glass fibre reinforced concrete, also known as GRC; in Russia it is called SFB (steklofibrobeton). According to TECHNOFORMA, it consists of four components — glass fibre, sand, high-grade cement and water; it is non-combustible (class NG under Russian standards), highly frost-resistant and can be cast into almost any shape.

Articles in this section explain how the material works and how it is made — by spray-up and vibration casting, its strength, density and weight and how these affect the load on a facade, and where its strengths and limitations lie. A separate series compares GFRC with fibre-reinforced concrete, polymer concrete, polyurethane, foam plastic, gypsum, architectural concrete and natural stone, so you can make an informed choice.
