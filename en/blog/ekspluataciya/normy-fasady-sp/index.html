Facade Standards: Key Russian SP Codes and GOST Standards

Updated: 2026-09-25 · TECHNOFORMA editorial team

Standards for facade works in Russia are a combination of federal laws, codes of practice (SP) and state standards (GOST) that set requirements for safety, fire protection, strength and thermal protection of the facade, and for how work is carried out and accepted. There is no single "facade law": the requirements are spread across documents in different fields.

The key thing for clients and designers is to understand which document covers which issue: fire safety determines permissible materials and systems, load codes govern the fixing calculation, thermal protection sets insulation thickness, and the rules for works define installation and acceptance. The current editions of documents must be checked as of the design date.

The article is written for architects, designers, technical supervision and general contractors. Related articles: Facade maintenance, standards and repair.

Key documents

Key documents on which facade design and construction rely:

Federal Law No. 384-FZ "Technical regulations on the safety of buildings and structures"General requirements for mechanical and fire safety and energy efficiency
Federal Law No. 123-FZ "Technical regulations on fire safety requirements"Fire hazard classes of structures and materials
Russian code SP 2.13130 "Fire protection systems. Ensuring fire resistance of protected facilities"Fire hazard requirements for external walls and facade systems
Russian code SP 20.13330 "Loads and actions"Wind, snow and dead loads for substructure calculation
Russian code SP 14.13330 "Construction in seismic areas"Additional requirements for seismic zones
Russian code SP 50.13330 "Thermal performance of buildings"Thermal resistance of building envelope elements
Russian code SP 70.13330 "Load-bearing and enclosing structures"Rules for execution and acceptance of work

Rainscreen facade systems additionally require a document confirming the system's suitability for use in construction — a technical assessment with an album of technical solutions. The editions of the codes are updated regularly, so the design refers to the current edition.

Fire safety requirements

Fire safety is the main constraint when choosing a facade system, especially for multi-storey and public buildings.

  • Structural fire hazard class (K0–K3). For the outer side of external walls, the codes set the required class depending on the building's fire resistance rating and structural fire hazard class. High-rise and many public buildings require K0 — the construction must not spread fire.
  • Confirming the class. The class of a facade system is determined by fire testing a fragment to the Russian standard GOST 31251 — the whole system is tested: cladding, substructure, insulation, wind barrier.
  • Combustibility of materials. The combustibility group of cladding and insulation (NG — non-combustible, G1–G4) is determined by combustibility tests. For rainscreen facades on taller buildings, non-combustible insulation and cladding are used.
  • Details. Fire breaks in the ventilated cavity and framing of openings with non-combustible elements — according to the system's technical solutions.

GFRC is a mineral material; according to manufacturers, including TECHNOFORMA Group, it is classed as non-combustible (NG). The class of a specific system is confirmed by documents for the system as assembled.

Loads

The substructure and cladding fixings are calculated for a combination of loads according to SP 20.13330:

  • Dead weight of cladding, rails and insulation — decisive for heavy materials (stone, GFRC, concrete panels).
  • Wind load — pressure and suction; depends on the wind zone, terrain type, height and facade zone. At corners and parapets peak suction values are higher, and fixing spacing is reduced there.
  • Snow and ice — for canopies, cornices and horizontal elements.
  • Thermal actions — expansion of rails, accommodated by sliding connections.
  • Seismic — in seismic areas according to SP 14.13330.

The load-bearing capacity of anchors in the actual substrate is confirmed by on-site pull-out tests; the design value is taken from their results with safety factors. Test results are included in the as-built documentation.

Thermal protection

Thermal protection of external walls is governed by the Russian code SP 50.13330 "Thermal performance of buildings". It sets:

  • the required thermal resistance of walls depending on the heating degree-days of the construction area and the building's use;
  • accounting for non-uniformities — brackets, anchors, reveals, junctions — through the reduced (effective) thermal resistance;
  • requirements for protecting building envelope elements from excess moisture;
  • sanitary requirements for the internal surface temperature.

For a rainscreen facade this means that insulation thickness is not chosen by a "standard 100 mm" rule but calculated for the specific region, taking into account the thermally conductive elements of the substructure. Brackets are installed with thermal break pads, and insulation is laid without gaps. More in the article on facade energy efficiency.

Quality control

Quality control of facade works has several levels:

  1. Incoming inspection of materials — checking data sheets, certificates, compliance with the design, appearance and geometry.
  2. In-process inspection — setting out, installation of anchors, brackets, rails, insulation and cladding with tolerance checks.
  3. Inspection of concealed work — anchoring, insulation, wind barrier, substructure before it is covered by cladding, with records drawn up.
  4. Testing — anchor pull-out, and if necessary checks of tightening and strength.
  5. Acceptance — checking facade geometry, joints, junctions and completeness of as-built documentation.

On capital construction projects, inspection is carried out by the contractor and the technical client (construction supervision), and for projects subject to state construction oversight, also by the oversight authorities. Tolerances for geometric deviations and the scope of documentation are defined by the design and the codes of practice for construction works.

FAQ

Which SP codes apply to facades in Russia?

The main ones are SP 2.13130 (fire safety), SP 20.13330 (loads and actions), SP 50.13330 (thermal performance), SP 70.13330 (execution and acceptance of work), and in seismic areas SP 14.13330. The current edition is checked as of the design date.

Which GOST standard governs facade systems?

The fire hazard of facade systems is determined by tests to the Russian standard GOST 31251. For cladding stone — GOST 9480. The suitability of a rainscreen system is confirmed by a technical assessment.

What is class K0 for a facade?

The fire hazard class of a construction that does not spread fire. It is confirmed by fire testing a fragment of the assembled facade system.

Does a rainscreen substructure need to be calculated?

Yes: the substructure is calculated for the dead weight of the cladding, wind and other loads under SP 20.13330, and the anchors are verified by on-site pull-out tests.

Which documents are checked at facade acceptance?

The design, as-built drawings, records of concealed work, anchor test results, material data sheets and certificates, and the document confirming suitability of the facade system.
