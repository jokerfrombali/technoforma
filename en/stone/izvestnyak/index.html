Limestone for Facades

Limestone is a traditional cladding stone used on many historic buildings. TECHNOFORMA Group supplies limestone for facades (tiles, profiled elements and dimension stone) and installs the cladding.

What limestone is

Limestone is a sedimentary rock composed mainly of calcite. It forms from the remains of marine organisms and chemical deposits, so it can be dense and uniform or porous with shell inclusions. Soft limestone is easy to cut and profile, which makes it convenient for decoration.

Is shell limestone a limestone?

Yes. Shell limestone (coquina) is a limestone made up mainly of shells and shell fragments cemented with calcium carbonate. It is of marine origin, formed on the beds of former warm seas. It is lighter and more porous than dense limestone; see the shell limestone page for details.

Types of facade limestone

White limestone

A light stone for classical and neoclassical facades; it combines well with GFRC decoration.

Dagestan limestone

Dense light and cream limestones from the North Caucasus; the company has extensive experience with them, see the Dagestan stone page.

Imported limestones

From stock of stones from India, Turkey, China and Iran, or to order.

Formats

Limestone facade tiles of various thicknesses, large slabs for ventilated facades, profiled elements such as cornices, string courses, window sills and steps. For plinths, dense varieties with low water absorption are selected and treated with a water repellent.

Stock and supply

TECHNOFORMA Group has been working with natural stone for more than 15 years: it imports stone from India, Turkey, China and Iran, keeps stock from these countries and supplies stone to order from all over the world. The stone yards and production base are located in the Moscow Region, and the company's warehouses in Novorossiysk, Makhachkala, Yekaterinburg and Novosibirsk make delivery to the regions easier. The equipment processes both soft stones (sandstone, shell limestone, dolomite, limestone) and hard ones. Measurements are taken by the company's own surveyors, while layouts and 3D visualisation are prepared by the design department.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send facade or interior drawings, photos of the building and your preferences for colour and texture.
  2. Estimate. We select stone from stock or to order and calculate areas, elements and logistics; our surveyors visit the site if needed.
  3. Design. 3D visualisation, a layout with pattern matching and slab marking, fixing details.
  4. Production. Sawing and surface finishing on our own equipment, quality control, marking according to the layout.
  5. Delivery. From the stone yards in the Moscow Region or via the company's regional warehouses.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division fixes the stone with adhesive or on a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure; designer's supervision is provided.

What affects the price

  • origin and grade of the stone: from stock or supplied to order;
  • slab thickness and format;
  • surface finish: polished, honed, bush-hammered, sawn, split;
  • number of non-standard elements: corners, reveals, profiles, steps;
  • installation method and working height;
  • volume and logistics.

We calculate the exact cost from drawings and quantities; installation is priced after a site inspection.

FAQ

Is limestone frost-resistant?

Dense varieties are; frost resistance depends on the quarry, so the stone is selected to suit the climate.

What is the difference between limestone and shell limestone?

Shell limestone is a type of limestone made of shells; it is more porous and lighter.

Do you supply Dagestan limestone?

Yes, the company has extensive experience with Dagestan stones.
