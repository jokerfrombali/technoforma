Natural stone for facades: supply and installation

TECHNOFORMA supplies natural stone for facades, plinths and interiors and carries out turnkey cladding. The company has worked with stone for more than 15 years: it imports stone from India, Turkey, China and Iran, keeps stock from these countries and brings in other stones to order from anywhere in the world. The production base and stone warehouses are near Moscow.

Stone types available

For natural stone facade and plinth cladding we select the stone to suit the task, climate and style of the building:

  • Travertine — a warm, porous texture, a classic for feature facades;
  • Limestone and shell limestone (coquina) — soft stones that are easy to work;
  • Dolomite and sandstone — for walls and plinths;
  • Marble — for portals, panels and interiors;
  • Granite and basalt — hard stones for plinths, steps and paving;
  • Onyx — a decorative stone for backlighting;
  • Dagestan stone — the company has extensive experience with it.

Our equipment processes both soft stones (sandstone, shell limestone, dolomite, limestone) and hard ones.

Natural or artificial stone

Artificial stone, flexible stone and stone-effect cladding tiles cost less up front, but they are different materials, with different durability, texture and frost behaviour. Natural stone does not fade and keeps its appearance over the years. If you need lightweight stone-effect mouldings, an alternative is GFRC with a stone texture.

Services

  • cladding design and 3D facade visualisation before purchase;
  • stone layout — pattern matching and slab marking;
  • site measurement by our own surveyors;
  • supply from stock or to order;
  • installation, including on ventilated facade substructures;
  • designer's supervision.

How an order works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send architectural drawings, a sketch or photos of the facade; if there is no design, our design department will prepare one.
  2. Quote. We calculate the range and volumes and agree the scope of work and delivery schedule.
  3. Design. Design engineers and 3D visualisers issue the working documentation, layout and fixing details.
  4. Production. Stone is selected from stock or ordered from a supplier; slabs are processed and marked according to the layout, with quality control at every stage.
  5. Delivery. Shipping from the production base near Moscow, via company warehouses if needed.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division of more than 100 specialists fits the substructure, insulation and cladding; installation supervision is also available.

What drives the cost

The price of natural facade stone per m² and the cladding rate in the estimate depend on:

  • stone type and origin;
  • slab thickness and surface finish;
  • layout complexity and the number of non-standard elements (corners, reveals, profiles);
  • installation method — adhesive or ventilated rainscreen facade substructure;
  • building height and site conditions;
  • volume and logistics.

The installation cost is determined after a site inspection.

FAQ

How much does natural facade stone cost per m²?

The price depends on stone type, thickness, finish and volume; installation is priced after a site inspection. Send us your drawings and we will prepare a quote.

Can I buy plinth cladding stone without installation?

Yes, stone is supplied without installation too; layout and measurement can be ordered separately.

Which stone is best for a plinth?

Dense stones with low water absorption suit plinths — granite, basalt, dense limestones and dolomites.

Do you import stone to order?

Yes. Besides stock from India, Turkey, China and Iran, the company supplies stone to order from anywhere in the world.

Send us drawings or photos of the facade — we will select the stone and quote the cladding.
