Travertine for Facades and Interiors

Travertine is one of the most recognisable facade stones, with its warm colour, characteristic pores and layered pattern. TECHNOFORMA Group supplies travertine for facades and interiors from stock and to order and carries out the cladding.

What travertine is

Travertine is a sedimentary rock, a calcareous tufa formed from mineral spring deposits. Its composition is close to limestone, but it differs in its porous texture and layering: the voids are left by gas and plant remains. Travertine is easy to cut and work, yet dense enough for cladding. Its texture ranges from calm and uniform to strongly banded; a seamless look is achieved by matching slabs in the layout.

Types and colours

By colour

Beige and ivory (light cream) are the most popular; also white, classic, noce (dark brown), grey, golden and reddish varieties.

By origin

Turkish and Iranian travertine differ in shade, density and pattern; the company imports stone from both countries. Armenian travertine is known on the market but is supplied to order.

By finish

Ground, polished, honed, aged (brushed, tumbled); pores can be filled or left open, and for facades the choice takes the climate into account.

Travertine for facades

On facades, travertine is used as slabs on a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure or fixed with adhesive to the substrate. In climates with frequent freeze-thaw cycles, stone density, slab thickness and water-repellent protection are important; these are built into the design. Travertine is also used for interiors, stairs, window sills and floors.

Travertine and imitations

Travertine-look porcelain stoneware, acrylic coatings and liquid travertine reproduce the pattern but not the depth and refinement of natural stone. If lightweight decoration with a stone texture is needed, natural travertine on flat surfaces can be combined with glass fibre reinforced concrete (GFRC) on complex profiles.

Stock and supply

TECHNOFORMA Group has been working with natural stone for more than 15 years: it imports stone from India, Turkey, China and Iran, keeps stock from these countries and supplies stone to order from all over the world. The stone yards and production base are located in the Moscow Region, and the company's warehouses in Novorossiysk, Makhachkala, Yekaterinburg and Novosibirsk make delivery to the regions easier. The equipment processes both soft stones (sandstone, shell limestone, dolomite, limestone) and hard ones. Measurements are taken by the company's own surveyors, while layouts and 3D visualisation are prepared by the design department.

How ordering works

  1. Drawings and brief. You send facade or interior drawings, photos of the building and your preferences for colour and texture.
  2. Estimate. We select stone from stock or to order and calculate areas, elements and logistics; our surveyors visit the site if needed.
  3. Design. 3D visualisation, a layout with pattern matching and slab marking, fixing details.
  4. Production. Sawing and surface finishing on our own equipment, quality control, marking according to the layout.
  5. Delivery. From the stone yards in the Moscow Region or via the company's regional warehouses.
  6. Installation. Our own installation division fixes the stone with adhesive or on a ventilated rainscreen facade substructure; designer's supervision is provided.

What affects the price

  • origin and grade of the stone: from stock or supplied to order;
  • slab thickness and format;
  • surface finish: polished, honed, bush-hammered, sawn, split;
  • number of non-standard elements: corners, reveals, profiles, steps;
  • installation method and working height;
  • volume and logistics.

We calculate the exact cost from drawings and quantities; installation is priced after a site inspection.

FAQ

How much does travertine facade cladding cost per m²?

The price depends on grade, origin, thickness and finish; the estimate is based on the facade drawings.

Is travertine suitable for exterior cladding in Russia?

Yes, with the right choice of density, thickness, protective impregnation and fixing details.

Which travertine is better: Turkish or Iranian?

They differ in shade and pattern; the choice is made from samples and visualisation.

Can I buy travertine without installation?

Yes, the stone is also supplied without installation.
